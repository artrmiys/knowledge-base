    Getting started¶

    Here: where the app is and how it updates, what you see on first launch, how to open the demo job and what to try in it, where to find commands (F1, palette) and where settings live. Needed on day one and after every update. To learn step by step: First steps: 10 lessons.

    Where the app is and how it updates¶

    Estimators run OurPlanCore Lite: no AI models and no terminal, lighter than the full version. How they differ: Lite vs full version.

    What Where
    The app itself a single file ourplancore.exe in the shared Dropbox folder Office Folder\programs
    Launch this file or a desktop shortcut to it
    Update a new file arrives in the same folder through Dropbox and replaces the old one; no previous version is kept next to it. What changed: the changes folder in the same place and What's new
    Excel templates the templates folder in the same place: TemplateCom.xlsm, TemplateResidential.xlsm
    Version in the window title: OurPlanCore 3.0.x-lite - <job>; the same in the first Version ... line of the log %LOCALAPPDATA%\OurPlanCore Lite\roaming\logs\app-<date>.log

    Jobs, settings and templates do not depend on the update: they are stored separately (see Where settings live).

    The full version is installed separately: the file ourplancore.exe in the Desktop\updates\OurPlanCore folder, with the Excel templates next to it; the window title shows the number without -lite, the log is in %APPDATA%\OurPlanCore\logs. When it is updated, the previous file stays next to it as .bak for rollback.

    Rolling back to .bak (full version)

    An older build reads crops (Crop to Sheet) and Hide masks as regular markup and loses their fields on save. If you rolled back, do not save a job with crops until you are back on a fresh build.

    First launch and the start screen¶

    Tile What it does
    Open a recent job list of recent jobs and job folders
    New blank job an empty job, sheets are added later
    New job from PDFs a job from a folder of PDF drawings
    Open sample job a demo job to get acquainted

    After that, work happens in Main View: Pages on the left, the sheet in the center, Takeoffs on the right, the tool strip at the bottom. Window map: on the section hub.

    Demo job: a tour¶

    The demo job is a training job with no client data. Try every button on it without worrying about breaking anything: it can be created again at any time. The First steps: 10 lessons course uses it too.

    How to open the demo job¶

    Why: to get a job with sheets, dots and a canonical tree where you can learn. You need: the app running; no job has to be open.

    1. Press the Open sample job tile on the start screen or open Main > Open / Import > Sample job (demo to explore); the same item is available as the Sample link at the bottom of the Open project... dialog.
    2. Wait for the status Sample project created: OurPlanCore Guide Sample.: the app creates the job in the jobs folder and opens it right away.
    3. In Pages, expand the 00. Guide folder and click a plan sheet.

    Result: the window title shows OurPlanCore 3.0.x - OurPlanCore Guide Sample, sheets on the left, the Takeoffs tree with ready-made takeoffs on the right, Scale: and Route: in the status bar.

    What is inside¶

    What Where to look Why
    00. Guide hint cards image sheets at the top of the Pages folder a map of the window, ribbons and panels right in the app
    A plan with the W dot, a structural plan with the F dot, a copy for shear walls with the S dot, a unit plan with the U dot sheets in 00. Guide, dots under the Scale header how a sheet set is split up (split by sheets)
    A canonical tree: sqfts, walls/1st floor walls, framing/1st floor framing/..., openings, shear walls, eves rakes Takeoffs panel takeoff names with numbers for Excel, folders by floor
    Roof outline rf 6 with eaves roof plan and sqfts, eves rakes roof area with the pitch in the name; in the full version, a starting point for the 3D roof
    A Start here... note and observations in the AI Inbox plan sheet; Inbox panel with the AI module (full version only) an example of markup and AI entries

    The exact contents of the demo after the 3.0.1 release should be checked against the job itself: takeoff and sheet names in the table are approximate.

    What to try, step by step¶

    1. Sheets and dots. Open a plan, read Route: W 1st floor in the status bar; press F2 and enter the same name again: the dot does not change. Make a copy of the sheet (Ctrl+D), rename it to ... sw, click the S dot. Split by sheets
    2. Routing. Press L, type the name ext 2x6 9.09, look at the destination line in the New Item dialog, make two clicks and C: the takeoff sits in walls/1st floor walls. Names and auto-routing
    3. Tools. On the same plan O on a door, A on a room, X to cut an area; on a structural sheet J for a joist area and B for a beam from support to support. Tools
    4. Legend. Display > Legend Show, then right-click the sheet, Organize > Sort Sheet Legend by Key; H on a takeoff hides it from the sheet, the dot next to the takeoff under the sheet in Pages brings it back. Takeoffs tree
    5. Crops and mask. Page > Blank Sheet, then Page > Crop to Sheet from the plan onto the blank sheet; Annotation > Hide with a box over what is not needed. Sheets: advanced
    6. 3D (full version only). Turn on the 3D module, select rf 6, the 3D tab, Roof Base, Auto, Iso. In Lite, skip this step. 3D roof
    7. Export. PDF Output > Preview, Main > Export of one sheet; in an open copy of TemplateCom.xlsm click a cell, select a takeoff and press Excel at the bottom (with the XL strip on, also the WL button). PDF and delivery, Export to Excel

    Broke the demo, what next¶

    Anything goes: delete takeoffs, move sheets, change dots, clean the tree with Delete Empty. To start fresh, press Open sample job again: the app creates a new copy with a unique name (OurPlanCore Guide Sample (2)), the old one stays in the jobs folder; you can delete it in File Explorer via Open in Explorer or keep it as your sandbox. The demo does not touch the app settings (Settings); your global rules stay yours.

    F1 and the command palette¶

    Key What it opens
    F1 a shortcut cheat sheet over the window; closes with Esc
    Ctrl+Shift+P command palette: categories File, View, Workspace, Tools, Edit, Pages, Sheets, Takeoffs and, in the full version, AI; type part of the name and Enter
    Ctrl+O Open / Import; Ctrl+Shift+O - recent jobs

    A command without a shortcut (for example Ends, Toggle Dark Theme, Hide / Show All Takeoffs) is run from the palette or gets a shortcut in Settings > Keyboard Shortcuts. Shortcuts work by physical keys in both the English and the Russian keyboard layout. Full list: Keyboard shortcuts.

    Theme and sheet look¶

    The Display ribbon:

    Button What it does
    Dark dark window theme
    Paper sheet paper tone: White, Soft gray, Medium gray, Dark gray, Warm, Soft green, Black
    Edge background around the sheet: White, Gray, Dark
    Static image, DPI the sheet as a single raster (on by default, 150 DPI); Black vector draws vectors in black
    Legend Show / Size / Pos measurement legend on the sheet
    Zoom mouse wheel step

    The dark theme is easier on the eyes in long sessions, but check takeoff colors before the PDF on white paper: the PDF prints on white.

    How to make your first measurement¶

    Why: to go from an open job to a saved takeoff in five minutes. You need: a job with named sheets (from intake or the demo).

    1. Press Ctrl+O and choose Open project... (jobs).
    2. In Pages expand 00. imported/Arch, open a floor plan, read Scale: in the status bar and the dot under the Scale header (sheets).
    3. Press L, name the takeoff ext 2x6 9.09, trace the exterior walls, press C (tools).
    4. Find in Takeoffs where the takeoff went: walls/1st floor walls with the W 1 dot (names and auto-routing).
    5. Select the takeoff, press Space, draw another run: Record continues the takeoff instead of creating a new one (tree).
    6. Press Ctrl+S and wait for Save: Saved hh:mm:ss.

    Result: a takeoff with two sections in walls/1st floor walls, Total in LFT, the job saved. Next, step by step: First steps: 10 lessons.

    What the entities mean¶

    Entity Role
    Project / Job the whole job: sheets, Takeoffs tree, job settings, source PDFs; one .ourplan file
    Page / Sheet a sheet with a scale, a W/F/S/U dot, a legend and overlays
    Takeoff item a named container for measurements of one type: Line, Area, Count, Joist Area
    Measurement geometry on a specific sheet; one takeoff can collect measurements from several sheets
    Annotation (markup) marks with no quantity: Highlight, Draw Line, Arrow, Box, Cloud, Note, Hide

    Where settings live¶

    What Where
    General settings, shortcuts, logs Lite: %LOCALAPPDATA%\OurPlanCore Lite\roaming (settings.json, logs folder); full version: %APPDATA%\OurPlanCore
    Rule presets (routing, shortcuts), caches Lite: %LOCALAPPDATA%\OurPlanCore Lite\local; full version: %LOCALAPPDATA%\OurPlanCore, terminal files there too
    Settings of a specific job inside the job in AI_Context/settings
    Jobs the jobs folder shown by Open project... (Manage job folders... adds folders)

    Lite has its own profile: its settings start from the built-in defaults and do not mix with the full version's settings. Each Settings section has Reset, Save global default and Save as this job: a rule can be overridden for one job without touching the global one. Which sections exist: Settings and modules. Some tabs (Sheet Manager, Takeoff Manager, Report Builder, Job Tracker) are off by default and are turned on in Settings > Modules; Materials, AI and 3D exist only in the full version. What they do and when they are needed: Extra features.
