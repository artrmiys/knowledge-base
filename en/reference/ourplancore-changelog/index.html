    OurPlanCore - what's new¶

    This page lists what went into OurPlanCore 3.0: the new 3.0 releases first, below them what accumulated in builds 2.2.6-2.2.42. You need it to see which feature was missing in an older version and where to read about it. Releases 3.0.4-3.0.26 are not written up here yet.

    3.1 (11/17/2026)¶

    • Groups: measurements of one sheet are collected into a group (Groups > Create Group... on the bottom strip), and its linked copies are pasted onto other sheets with Ctrl+V, choosing By sheet router or Source takeoffs. Edit Group and Apply to all change the contents of every copy, Route Instance by Sheet moves the takeoffs of one copy, and there are Make Unique and Ungroup Instance. A group adds no quantity of its own. Do not save a job with groups in a version before 3.1. Also in Lite. Groups
    • Routing top: a checkbox on the Viewport ribbon moves the W/F/S/U dots from under Scale to the upper-right corner of the ribbon, visible on every tab. Routing top
    • Random Colors for Items on a folder also recolors takeoffs with a starting color from their name, except the fixed rules (bft); the new color differs from the previous one. Takeoff colors
    • The version number is short now: 3.1. Lite no longer shows the -lite tail in the window title (how to tell Lite).

    3.0.37 (11/16/2026)¶

    • Sheet pictures in the disk cache carry a render version. Pictures made by earlier versions (for example, a filled form without its fields at low zoom) are no longer used, and the sheet is rendered again. That is why the first opening of a sheet after the update can take a second longer; after that it is as usual. Troubleshooting

    3.0.36 (11/16/2026, Lite only)¶

    • The XL strip can be turned on in Lite: Settings > Modules, the Excel Strip (XL) checkbox, off by default. In the full version the same checkbox can hide the strip. Lite and full version

    3.0.35 (11/16/2026)¶

    • Filled PDF forms: typed values and check marks of form fields are visible on the sheet, in thumbnails and in the exported PDF, as in Acrobat. Before, a filled form looked empty. Ordinary drawings without forms did not change.

    3.0.34 (11/14/2026)¶

    • A blank sheet after Change Sheet Size... no longer shows the old picture of the previous size. Blank sheets
    • A sheet with crops picked as an overlay shows its crops in their frames and with their opacity, with the tint, opacity and offset of the overlay layer. Overlay

    3.0.33 (11/12/2026)¶

    • A sheet overlay is drawn above Crop to Sheet crops, on screen and in PDF. The Hide mask, measurements and markups stay above the overlay.

    3.0.32 (11/12/2026)¶

    • A job opens faster, moving sheets and takeoffs in the trees is faster.
    • A double-click on the colored takeoff glyph in the tree or on the active takeoff line opens its properties. Takeoffs tree

    3.0.31 (11/06 and 11/11/2026)¶

    • Dragging and ordering sheets in the Pages tree is faster; undo of a move saves only the affected sheets.
    • Full version only: DeepSeek in the terminal finds rules and lessons with the same context search as Claude and Codex. AI console

    3.0.30 (11/05/2026)¶

    • TemplateCom.xlsm template: the formula for the balcony and porch block size now reads the same on a Russian and an English computer (Two languages). The numbers in the template did not change.
    • Writing to a closed workbook: a timeout is now reported as usual; before, it could end in a thread error.

    3.0.29 (11/04/2026)¶

    • Auto Link: a button next to Link to Excel, DFL only. One click links Sure takeoffs (the label in J equals the name, the number matches) and Probable ones (the number matches one to one in column K, with no name) right away. Cell values do not change, the workbook is not saved. Windows, doors and other openings, joists, unnamed points, small unnamed whole numbers are skipped. Ambiguous ones are linked in the result window, where Undo This Auto Link also is. Look at the result window right away: a Probable can land on someone else's row with the same number. Also in Lite. Auto Link
    • Reset All Links: removes all links of the job after a prompt, cannot be undone, the values in the cells stay. Names leave open workbooks right away, closed ones at the next opening or right away with a second button. Reset All Links
    • Unlink for a closed workbook: the hidden name now goes away when the workbook is opened while this job is open, instead of staying until Remove All Links.

    3.0.28 (11/04/2026)¶

    • Link to Excel: a button next to Value and Excel writes the number of one takeoff into the active cell of the open workbook and keeps it there. The link goes only from OurPlanCore to Excel; inserting rows does not break it. A closed workbook is updated on Ctrl+S or with Update Links. States Linked, Pending, Conflict, Broken, the All Excel Links... list, settings Settings > Excel Links. Before the workbook goes to the client the links are removed: Remove All Links from This Workbook.... Also in Lite. Link to Excel

    3.0.27 (11/04/2026)¶

    • PDF export by default puts measurements in as separate objects that can be edited in Adobe Acrobat; the look stays as in OurPlanCore. In Acrobat lines, areas and labels move; Count points and joist areas do not, and the legend is the same type as the points and should not move (it was not tested separately). Adobe does not recalculate labels and the legend. To turn it off: PDF Output > ADOBE > Editable, settings Settings > PDF Editable Export. Also in Lite. Editing the PDF in Adobe

    3.0.3 (10/18/2026)¶

    • The sheet legend is split by default into blocks by the current Takeoffs folders. On framing Posts, Beams, Details come first; SQFTs after the other measurements. The order and names are edited in Settings > Sheet Legend globally or per job. The button next to the crop lock turns off the automatic blocks on one sheet; manual groups stay available. Sheet legend
    • On an F sheet, a Count/Points ending in Post goes to framing/<floor>/posts, a name ending in Stair with any tool goes to framing/<floor>/stairs. Names and auto-routing
    • Crop to Sheet places crops from the top left corner to the right, then below, with margins and without overlap. If a whole crop does not fit, it is not added; the status suggests a larger or a new sheet. Crops
    • On an error, autosave retries after 30, 60 and 120 seconds, then every 5 minutes until it succeeds; the intervals are edited in Settings > Autosave Recovery. A temporarily unreadable file is checked every 30 seconds; the protection is lifted only after the data loads successfully. A corrupted file stays protected for manual recovery. Saving

    3.0.2 (10/17/2026)¶

    Fixes found while recording the clips for this section. Rollback: the ourplancore.exe.3.0.1-*.bak file next to the EXE.

    • Fit (F) puts the sheet in the middle of the work area, not in the top left corner.
    • Autosave: if the job file changed while the app was writing the .ourplan, it retries by itself. Before, such a race stopped autosave until a manual Ctrl+S (Save: Failed). Saving
    • A sheet file that is briefly busy with another write is reread by the app instead of being declared Save: Protected data right away.
    • 3D: the rf 6, rf 4 roof area sits on top of the walls; before, the pitch digit was read as a floor number, and the slab hung several floors higher. Already saved models are fixed on opening.
    • 3D roof: any sheet tool turns off edge selection; Ctrl or Shift with a right-click add an edge without the sheet menu; Auto and Wall no longer break Roof Base groups. 3D roof
    • A large sheet no longer stays white after the 3D tab, and undo (Ctrl+Z) survives switching to the 3D tab and back.

    3.0.1 (10/17/2026)¶

    Fixes found while shooting the screenshots and clips for this section on the sample job. Rollback: the ourplancore.exe.3.0.0-*.bak file next to the EXE.

    • Open sample job works again. The sample is laid out as in real work: sheet copies with the W, F, S and U dots, a tree per the canon, each takeoff on its own sheet, a gable roof from sqfts/rf 6, the saved 3D model visible right away. Sample job
    • The default tree folder is basement floor walls (was basement foor walls).
    • New Takeoff Item: the measurement type shows Line, not the record text.
    • A new blank sheet gets a W/F/S/U dot from its name; Duplicate Page gives the copy a unique name and does not carry over the dot.
    • PDF Output > Preview keeps the sheet's legend groups.
    • Batch Overlay: column headers in English, with tooltips.
    • Ctrl+Z from the ribbon also undoes a Hide mask that was just drawn.
    • The XL strip and the command strip scroll with the wheel in a short window.
    • The tiles of the start card respond to a normal click.

    3.0.0 (10/17/2026)¶

    Release 3.0.0 is build 2.2.42 plus one fix: the Workspace sync no longer gets stuck on a dialog transcript that is too large (a file over 256 MB is skipped with a warning, the other dialogs sync). Window title: OurPlanCore 3.0.0 - <job>. One install in the updates folder Desktop\updates\OurPlanCore, the rollback is the .bak file next to the EXE. Below is everything that accumulated since 2.2.7 and went into 3.0.0; what 2.2.6 and 2.2.7 themselves brought (a single .ourplan file, autosave, Project Data Recovery, live Preview, key editor) is in the 08/15-09/06 section.

    Where a new takeoff goes¶

    • W/F/S/U dots under the Scale label: one dot per sheet decides the folder and floor for any tool; status Route: W 2nd. The dot is set automatically from the sheet name (a is W, s is F, a final u is U), and there is a Route sheets without a dot now pass.
    • Takeoff Name Routing: rules by name and tool (eve, rake, gable, prpt, trussheel, Openings) beat the sheet dot.
    • Green and red dots in the Takeoffs header: the building for new takeoffs; New takeoffs paste goes into it, copies keep their subfolders (D3/sqfts to D4/sqfts).
    • Takeoff Colors: bft is always light gray; random colors avoid gray, black and white.
    • Details: Takeoff routing.

    Editing measurements¶

    • Merge and Split of whole takeoffs from the tree (Ctrl+M, Ctrl+Shift+M): by section or by sheet, undo with Ctrl+Z in Takeoffs.
    • Break (Ctrl+Shift+B) and Break H/V (Ctrl+Shift+V): lines into segments, horizontal and vertical ones into separate takeoffs. Ends: Count marks at the segment ends.
    • H hides the takeoff of the selected segment, the highlighter moved to Shift+H. Keys work in the Russian layout.
    • Details: Editing takeoffs, Hotkeys.

    Sheets¶

    • Crop to Sheet: details from several sheets onto one clean sheet at 100 % scale; rotate, mirror, opacity, order, lock, the not true scale badge.
    • Blank Sheet with size, units and presets; Change Sheet Size...; fast zoom on large blank sheets.
    • Hide: a white mask over the sheet, prints in the PDF. Area markup by box. PDF Output > Opac is the line opacity in the PDF.
    • Legend groups and Sort Sheet Legend by Key (before space). Batch Overlay for all floors at once.
    • Sheet names across the whole set: tags rcp, demo, u, base, sch, in el, fin; OCR for scans; AI from the title block image; learning (the Kept column); sorting by the last word, rcp and demolition folders.
    • Details: Sheets: Crop to Sheet, Overlay, legend, Hide, Pages sheets.

    Excel and PDF¶

    • The XL strip: new buttons SH, SW, SHF, UN, BL and the framing buttons FR, PS, BM, HD, JS, DT, ST; Framing no longer wipes the template, a repeat run works; details get M flags.
    • The "comments" dot on the Excel button: takeoff and measurement notes go into the export.
    • PDF: crops and masks print even without Markups, legend groups in the PDF, export by building through the pipeline.
    • Details: Excel export, PDF export.

    AI, workspace, pipeline¶

    • Claude and Codex terminals in the window (>_) with live sheet context and Quick / Balanced / Deep effort.
    • Agent Bridge: an agent edits the open job through the app, with undo.
    • Workspace: a shared server for rules, skills, jobs and dialogs for a second computer.
    • AI Crop Notes: notes from a schedule in compact rows.
    • The In Progress button (Job Tracker module), importing an OurPlanCore job into the open one, the OurPlanCoreCli pipeline console.
    • Details: AI console, AI in the app, Workspace and modules, Managers.

    Settings¶

    Settings grew from 12 to 20 categories (Blank Sheets, Sheet Takeoff Routing, Takeoff Name Routing, Takeoff Colors, AI Crop Notes, Agent Bridge, Terminals, Workspace); 19 modules (plus Job Tracker). One principle everywhere: Reset, Save global default, Save as this job. Details: Settings: all categories.

    08/15-09/06: builds 2.2.6 and 2.2.7 Preview¶

    Until 2.2.8 the app ran on the stable 2.2.5 (07/22), and new work was built into a separate Preview with its own profile and shortcut. Since 2.2.10 there is no Preview: everything is installed into the main updates folder. What went in over those three weeks:

    Jobs and saving¶

    • A single .ourplan file (08/17): the job became a portable package; on opening it is unpacked into a working copy, and Save packs it back. Old folder jobs open with the same Open project.... Jobs and saving
    • Save As with a format choice (08/20): a new portable file or a compatible folder copy; since 09/01 it is a single Save As... item (Ctrl+Shift+S) that keeps the format of the open job. Jobs
    • Unified job storage (09/01): one Open project... dialog with source folders, a filter and Open project file... for an .ourplan outside those folders; the Settings > Project Storage category with size analysis and Compact snap.json. Jobs, Settings
    • Package autosave (09/02): the .ourplan file is repacked by itself after a few seconds of quiet and on Ctrl+S; status Save: Saved / Pending / Saving... / Failed; a retry when the file is temporarily locked. Autosave
    • Data protection (2.2.6 Preview, 09/06): a corrupted or locked metadata file is not overwritten with an empty one, Open / Import > Project Data Recovery... with Retry reading and Restore from copy...; a log of sheet operations and Undo Last Page Sort / Undo Last Page Operation; raster caches are read on long paths too. If the job did not save

    Sheets¶

    • Ctrl+Z brings back deleted sheets and folders in Pages (08/17). Sheets
    • Guided PDF metadata analysis (08/20): Metadata > Analyze PDF Metadata goes through one typical sheet of the set and asks you to show the title block areas; the Crop Hints button draws three areas, Sheet #, Sheet Title, Scale, which the app uses to find names and scales on the other sheets. Managers, Sheet Manager

    Measurements and output¶

    • Live PDF Output > Preview (09/05): a modeless window of the current sheet that redraws when the ribbon settings change; Fit, -, +, 100%, wheel zoom, right-button pan, Save PDF.... PDF and delivery
    • Tools in detached sheet windows (09/05): Beam and Openings work there too, paste and Ctrl+Z are shared between the main and the detached window. Other, detached windows
    • Repeat Line and Repeat Beam (09/05): the arrow next to the Line and Beam buttons, a series of segments or beams until Esc. Tools
    • Beam dimension offset and a line along the beam: Settings > Defaults > Beam. Settings
    • Move joist note (09/05): a checkbox in the Joist Area properties, the zone table can be dragged; the position is saved and printed. Tools, J Area
    • Paste between sheets with a choice of Same takeoffs / New takeoffs / Cancel (2.2.7, 09/06). Copying

    Keys and settings¶

    • Customizable keys (2.2.7 Preview, 09/06): a separate Settings > Keyboard Shortcuts editor with search, sequences, conflicts, JSON presets, a global and a per-job set, Recover settings.... Hotkeys
    • Faster Save and closing of large jobs, stable raster after zoom (2.2.7).
