    OurPlanCore 3.0¶

    OurPlanCore is an app for taking off quantities from PDF drawings: sheet, scale, measurement, Takeoffs tree, then export to the Excel DFL and PDF. Here: the window map, how this section is organized, and the path from an open job to delivery.

    This section describes OurPlanCore Lite, the build estimators have: no AI models and no terminal, simpler and lighter. AI models, the terminal, 3D and the XL strip exist only in the full version; they are grouped in a separate menu block "AI, terminal and 3D (full version)" or marked on the page. Item-by-item differences: Lite vs full version.

    • Start with the course

      First steps: 10 lessons on the demo job: open it, name a sheet, draw walls and openings, check, export to Excel and PDF. An hour of work; after that the rest of the section reads as a reference.

    • Try it on the demo job

      Main > Open / Import > Sample job (demo to explore) or the Open sample job tile on the start screen: a job with no client data that you can break and create again. Demo tour.

    • Lite or full version

      Lite vs full version: where to get Lite, how big it is, what it lacks and how to tell which version is open.

    # Area What is there Details
    1 Command ribbons Main, Page, Annotation, PDF Output, Viewport, Display; below them the mode row Main View, Settings and the manager tabs (visible when their modules are on) Getting started
    2 Pages panel tree of folders and sheets, buttons + New, Setup, Name, Scale, Name+Scale, search; sub-tabs PDF Layers, Overlay, Bookmarks Sheets
    3 Sheet tabs of open sheets and the drawing itself; under the Scale header the W/F/S/U dots and the crop lock Sheets, Tools
    4 Takeoffs panel green and red root dots, the active takeoff row with Record, Total, New Item, tabs Takeoffs, Estimating, Templates Takeoffs tree, Multiple buildings
    5 Bottom tool strip row 1: Pan, Select, Scale, Ruler, Count, Line, Area, J Area, Beam, Open, Cut, Snap, Ortho, Box; row 2: Transform, Scale: 1:N, Presets, Fit Tools, Editing and snaps
    6 XL strip (full version only) Excel export buttons: ALL, LG, SF, WL ... ST; next to them the >_ button of the AI console. Lite has neither this strip nor the button; Excel goes through the buttons at the bottom (Excel, Value, Link to Excel) Export to Excel, AI console
    7 Status bar <sheet>, Scale:, Zoom:, Mode:, Takeoff: ... (Route: W 1st), Save: Saved hh:mm:ss, the last command's message Jobs and saving

    The Takeoff: field shows where the next measurement will go, Save: shows whether the job is saved. These two are worth checking more often than the rest.

    Path: open a job, deliver the work¶

    1. Open the job. Main > Open / Import > Open project... or the Open a recent job tile on the start screen. The job already comes with sheet names and W/F/S/U dots. Jobs
    2. Check the sheets. Expand 00. imported/Arch, open a floor plan, check Scale: in the status bar and the dot under the Scale header (Route: W 1st). Name unnamed sheets (Page N) by hand and set the scale with S. Copies for shear walls get the S dot by hand. Sheets, Split by sheets
    3. Takeoffs tree. More > Auto Tree creates the COM template roots; several buildings: a folder for each, the green dot on the current one. Tree, Multiple buildings, tree canon
    4. Measurements by scope. One scope per pass over the sheet: walls L, openings O, areas A, joists J, beams B, posts and holdowns P. Space continues the selected takeoff. Tools, takeoff order in the workflow
    5. Takeoff names. The name carries the numbers for Excel: ext 2x6 9.09, 4.3x7.8 d, rf 6, eve 24 main. A new takeoff goes into a folder by itself, based on the sheet dot and the name. Names and auto-routing, name formats
    6. Check. The Estimating tab, Total on a takeoff, the sheet legend (Organize > Sort Sheet Legend by Key), H hides finished work, Delete Empty removes empty takeoffs. Tree
    7. Excel. Open the building's workbook made from TemplateCom.xlsm (the templates folder in Dropbox), click a cell, select a takeoff or folder in Takeoffs and press Excel or Value at the bottom; a number that should follow the measurement goes in with the Link to Excel button; run the calculation from the J:L list with the E-Wood tab buttons in Excel. In the full version the XL strip does the same section by section (ALL, the LG legend). Export to Excel, DFL workbook, E-Wood tab
    8. PDF and delivery. Ctrl+S, hide what is not needed (H, Hide mask), PDF Output > Preview, then Main > Export: only sheets with measurements, a separate PDF per building. Then the processed xlsx and Changes.txt for a revision. PDF and delivery, delivery

    What appeared in two months¶

    If you worked in a version from before mid-August, here is what changed in everyday actions. Full list by build: What's new.

    Before Now Where to read
    a job was a folder of files, you copied the whole thing one .ourplan file, autosave, status Save: Saved hh:mm:ss; Project Data Recovery if it gets damaged Jobs and saving
    a new takeoff was sorted into floors by hand the W/F/S/U dot on a sheet is set from the sheet name and sorts new takeoffs by itself; the Route: field in the status bar Split by sheets, Names and auto-routing
    eve, prpt, gable, Openings landed anywhere rules by name and tool (Settings > Takeoff Name Routing) Routing: settings
    one Takeoffs root green and red dots pick the building for new takeoffs; pasting copies mirrors the folders Multiple buildings
    H was a marker H hides a takeoff on the sheet, the marker moved to Shift+H Takeoffs tree
    Merge and Split for segments only Merge and Split of whole takeoffs from the tree, Break and Ends for lines Editing and snaps
    Excel by hand, piece by piece XL strip: ALL and buttons by section, framing, LG legend (full version only); the "comments" dot on the Excel button Export to Excel
    PDF set up blind live PDF Output > Preview, Opac for lines PDF and delivery
    PDF fixed only by a new export lines, areas and labels can be moved in Adobe Acrobat PDF and delivery
    the number in the workbook was updated with Value after every edit Link to Excel keeps the number in the cell by itself Export to Excel
    details were hunted down sheet by sheet Crop to Sheet collects details onto a clean sheet, the Hide mask removes the rest Sheets: advanced
    sheet names were checked one by one Name+Scale for the whole set; in the full version also OCR of scans, title block reading by an AI model, Crop Hints Sheets, Managers
    shortcuts were hard-coded Settings > Keyboard Shortcuts editor, shortcuts work with the Russian keyboard layout Keyboard shortcuts
    AI help only through crops in the full version, Claude and Codex terminals in the window (>_), Agent Bridge, a shared Workspace for two computers AI console, Workspace
