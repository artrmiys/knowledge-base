    Extra features¶

    This section collects what OurPlanCore 3.0 has but everyday E-Wood measuring almost never needs: snapping and tracing, manager tabs, bookmarks, the calculator, importing old jobs. 3D and AI are in the full version only and are described in its block. The "Every day" and "Advanced" level pages give these features one line with a link here, so they do not distract from walls, areas and framing. If you need one of these features, everything is described on the pages of this level.

    What is here and why¶

    Selection criteria (owner's decision, 10/17/2026):

    1. The module is off by default in Settings > Modules: Sheet Manager, Takeoff Manager, Report Builder, Materials, AI, 3D, Job Tracker. Without turning the module on, there are no buttons or tabs. Materials, AI and 3D are in the full version only, Lite does not have them at all (Lite vs full version).
    2. The feature is not in the measuring work rules. None of the skills for walls, openings, framing, roofing, shear walls and units mentions Snap, Ortho, Box, Layer Trace, Wall Trace, bookmarks, the calculator, detached windows, tree templates or PlanSwift import. The measuring canon rests on Line, Area, Count, J Area, Beam, Openings, Record, sheet dots, the export to Excel (in the full version, the XL strip) and Export PDF.
    3. A newer path replaced the feature. Report Builder gave way to the XL strip and the Excel and Link to Excel buttons, AI 3D Massing was replaced by the deterministic roof from edges, inserting PDF Takeoffs and PlanSwift is needed only for old jobs.

    What stays on its own levels: the Line, Area, Count, J Area, Beam, Openings, Pitch, Ruler, Record tools and copying; W/F/S/U dots; export to Excel; Export PDF and Preview; editing and snapping as one set (Cut, Merge, Split, Break, Ends, Combine, Similar, P Line, Snap, Ortho, Box) on the Editing and snapping page; Crop to Sheet, the Hide mask, Overlay, legend groups; Settings. The 3D roof, the AI console, Agent Bridge and Workspace are described in the full version block, starting with AI in the app.

    How to turn a module on¶

    1. Settings > Modules: check the module, Apply (until restart) or Save global default; Save as this job turns the module on only in the open job.
    2. The presets Current default, All modules on, Takeoff Only, Minimal set the whole set at once; Reset restores the built-in one.
    3. A module that is on adds its tab to the mode bar (Sheet Manager, Takeoff Manager, Report Builder; in the full version also Materials, AI Manager, 3D) or buttons to the ribbon (Name, Scale, Name+Scale, In Progress).
    4. A disabled module hides its buttons and tab; a key for a hidden command does not fire.

    The full module list with groups: Settings, Modules.

    Rare items of the measurement menu on the sheet¶

    Right-click on a measurement on the sheet opens the edit menu; copying, Merge, Split and other common items are described on the Editing and snapping page. Here are the items you need rarely:

    Item What it does When you need it
    Takeoff Properties... the properties window of the whole takeoff, same as right-click on the takeoff in Takeoffs change the name, color or Notes of the takeoff without looking for it in the tree
    Section Properties... (for Count, Count Properties...) name and Notes of one measurement (section); for a measurement with no takeoff in the tree, the item is gray and called Measurement Properties... label one piece of a takeoff, for example per S2.1
    Set / Reset Joist Direction on an Area: the joist direction by two clicks on this measurement turn the joist layout in one area
    Set Direction for All Areas on an Area: the direction is drawn once by two clicks on this measurement and applied to all Area measurements of this takeoff on all sheets; the takeoff becomes a joist takeoff many floor framing areas with one direction, like J Area

    The joist items show on an Area with the Advanced Takeoff Tools module on (on by default); the same two items are in the right-click menu of a takeoff in Takeoffs. Notes comments go to Excel when the dot on the Excel button is on (comments in Excel).

    Pages of this level¶

    • Snap and tracing

      The PDF Layers tab and Layer Trace; Trace Walls Inside Area; trace requests through AI Inbox (full version). The snaps themselves (Snap, PDF Snap, Ortho, Box) together with measurement editing: Editing and snapping.

    • Managers

      Sheet Manager, Takeoff Manager, Report Builder, Job Tracker: tabs turned on in Settings > Modules; Materials and AI Manager in the full version.

    • AI, terminal and 3D (full version)

      AI in the app, AI console, 3D roof, 3D and Massing: Lite does not have these.

    • Other

      Bookmarks, Quick Calculator, detached windows and Tile M2, Similar across all sheets, Templates, PlanSwift and PDF Takeoffs import.
