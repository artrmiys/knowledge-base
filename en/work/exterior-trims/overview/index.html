    Exterior Trims¶

    Exterior Trims are wood, PVC or composite linear stock and products on the outside of the building: casing around openings, corner boards, band and watertable, head crowns, soffit and fascia, frieze, columns, porch, deck and balcony trim. It is a separate scope: not siding, not EIFS, stucco or veneer, and not framing. We count only the trim material and only when trim is in the job.

    What we do not count

    EIFS, stucco, stone and brick veneer are finish systems by others. Canonical page: EIFS / Stucco / Veneer; the rule for the trim scope and J-channel: Exclusions and J-Channel.

    What is included and in what order¶

    • Casing, Corner & Band

      Ext. casing (sides, heads, apron, mulls), corner boards, band and watertable, head crowns with cap and flashing, shutters, louvers, brackets.

      Casing, Corner & Band

    • Furring & Window Jambs

      Furring 1x4 / 2x4 under siding and jamb 2x4 / 2x8 / 2x10 in openings. They live in the sections, not on the elevations.

      Furring & Jambs

    • Soffit & Fascia

      Soffit and fascia along eaves and rakes, frieze, rake moulding, returns, columns.

      Soffit & Fascia

    • Porch / Deck / Balcony

      Porch trims, flare; hub for railing, balcony layers, pergola.

      Porch / Deck / Balcony

    • Rails & Decking

      Canonical page for railing: composite as a system, wood by components, metal by others; decking, post hardware, lattice.

      Rails & Decking

    • Balcony build-up

      All balcony layers: what the frame counts, what the trim counts; assembly with pavers.

      Balcony build-up

    • Outdoor Shower & Pergola

      IPE privacy screen, pergola, trellis, canopy.

      Shower & Pergola

    • Trim macros

      C_TrimsCalc, collapsing duplicates, inserting jambs, pavers, balcony, bolts blocks.

      Trim macros

    • Exclusions and J-Channel

      What we do not count in the trim scope and where J-channel goes.

      Exclusions

    Workflow¶

    1. Read the siding material note on the elevations: Hardi, vinyl, cedar, EIFS, stone. It decides which trim is possible at all (vinyl often means J-channel instead of casing).
    2. Open the window and door details, wall sections, eave details: profiles, furring, jambs, head crown with cap and flashing.
    3. Count by groups: openings (casing), corners and bands, overhangs, porch and balcony, products (shutters, louvers, columns).
    4. Size not specified: TBD and the note Note: All trims to be verified. Scaled per elevations. Do not make up a product.

    How to measure in OurPlanCore¶

    The trims root in the building tree. The physical object sets the takeoff type: casing, band, corner and fascia are Lines, one item per profile (2" casing, 8" horizontal trim); soffit and the porch ceiling are Areas along the real outline; shutters, columns, brackets are Counts. Each takeoff has its own color and its own point symbol. Elevations and RCPs have no sheet dot, so check where the item landed. Eave and rake lengths come from eves rakes, the siding area from siding.

    DFL row¶

    A Description B Size C Qty D Unit E
    Ext Casing 5/4x4 =CEILING(G*1.1,10) LFT A601
    Crowns 3-1/2" crown =CEILING(G*1.1,10) LFT scaled
    Corner Boards 5/4x6 =EVEN(G*2*1.1) LFT A201
    Soffits at Porch Beadboard =CEILING(G*1.1,10)*units SQ FT 6/A550
    Columns 12" Dia Fbg Non-taper 4 8' w/ cap & base
    Shutters 20"x60" paneled 12 pairs A201
    • G is the measured length or area from the tree. The 1.1 waste and the round-up are in the C formula: to 10, or to 2 when the total is under 50.
    • Corner Boards: G is the sum of the outside corner heights. A corner made of two boards: =EVEN(G*2*1.1), so 8 corners at 28' = 224 LFT of corners give 494 LFT of boards. A one-piece L profile per the detail: =EVEN(G*1.1).
    • Write the labels as the DFL template does: Ext Casing, Corner Boards, Crowns, Frieze Board, Over Fascia Trims, Fascia at Eves, Soffits at Eves, Watertable Trim, Window Sills. The macros sum rows by the label text: CornerBoards and Corner Boards are two different rows to them.
    • LFT for linear stock, SQ FT for panels and beadboard, pcs and pairs for products. For Columns D holds the column height (8') and C the count; the height is not written inside the description.
    • Size and profile always visible (5/4x4, 3-1/2" crown, 4" Vinyl corner); never write a generic trim.
    • In the trims workbook for macros a row is three columns: Label, Value, Unit, see Trim macros.

    Common mistakes¶

    • Trim counted when the scope is Frame only.
    • Vinyl siding: J-channel and wood casing on the same openings.
    • Canopy and porch counted again in eave and rake; porch SQFT empty on the Front Page.
    • A product guessed instead of TBD and a note.
    • All mistakes: Excel DFL and notes, Deck, porch, balconies.
